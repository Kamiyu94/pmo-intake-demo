# Generic demonstration categories

- **A — New capability or investment:** a new platform, system, or major capability outside an already approved active project.
- **B — Existing system enhancement:** a change to an operational system's function, workflow, report, or interface.
- **C — Active project change:** a change to the scope, delivery, design, integration, or acceptance of an ongoing project. Confirm the project relationship.
- **Service request:** incidents, access, equipment, or routine support within an existing service.
- **Informational:** an update with no requested action.
- **Possibly related:** evidence suggests a relationship; human confirmation is needed.
- **Insufficient information:** the request may be valid but key facts are missing. Do not treat this as rejection.
- **Out of scope:** unrelated content rather than an IT demand.

Classification, assignment, investment approval, and prioritization are separate decisions. These examples are not an organization's approved operating policy.
